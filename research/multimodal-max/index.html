The leg architecture

Whole-leg orientation

A pitch joint rotates the leg at the chassis, supporting the transition between different postures.

Five-bar positioning

Two coordinated inputs position the leg through a closed-chain linkage.

Differential wrist & swerve

The lower mechanism combines wrist articulation, wheel steering, and wheel rotation. These components are included in the current CAD.

Research direction & development

My role

I defined the research direction and engineering priorities, led project development, and mentored the team through design decisions. My hands-on contribution includes the first servo-driven whole-leg pitch joint, with adjustable tensioners and eccentric spacers.

During my final semester at Georgia Tech, I will continue working on the electronics, research direction, hardware builds, and experimentation.

Noah Veron

Noah developed the remaining detailed CAD, including the five-bar mechanism, and is carrying the project forward with my continued mentorship.

From CAD to hardware

The complete assembly is now available to explore. Hardware integration and experimental validation remain in progress; the motion study shows intended kinematics, not measured robot performance.
