Full-scale wheeled-legged robot

A robot concept for changing between mammalian and arachnid leg postures, combining wheeled mobility with an articulated leg architecture. The intended robot is nearly 600 mm long.

I managed the project, led ideation, and set the engineering direction. I designed the first pitch axis and guided much of the subsequent development. Noah Veron developed the remaining CAD and is completing the project with my continued mentorship.

Full chassis layout

The nearly 600 mm chassis brings four leg-drive modules around a central structural frame. The circular side assemblies support whole-leg pitch; they are not the robot’s wheels.

The lower legs, wheels, and feet will connect to this structure and are still in development. Belts are not shown.
