My contribution

What I designed and built.

Mechanical architecture, model-based control, and the integrated prototype.

01

Complete mechanical design

I designed the full SolidWorks assembly: the four-bar leg mechanisms, actuator and bearing packaging, chassis, wheel assemblies, and component mounts. I also developed fabrication drawings for the hardware.

CAD assembly · Mechanisms · Fabrication
02

Engineering analysis

I first used a mock robot dog to study leg motion and spec initial joint torques. I later used the full CAD-derived URDF to derive the rigid-body dynamics: the joint-space inertia matrix, Coriolis matrix, and gravity vector. Torque estimates and FEA guided motor sizing and structural reinforcement.

Kinematics · Motor sizing · FEA
03

Impedance controller

I developed the joint-space impedance controller, combining stiffness and damping feedback with inverse-dynamics feed-forward torque. The robot model supplies inertia, Coriolis, and gravity terms so the feedback can shape a compliant response.

Model-based feed-forward · Stiffness · Damping
04

Hardware integration & electrical revisions

I integrated the actuators, battery, power distribution, CAN wiring, compute, LiDAR, and depth camera. I also revised component specifications and added bulk and local decoupling capacitors to address power-integrity needs.

Packaging · Component selection · Power integrity

Developed controller

Impedance feedback + inverse-dynamics feed-forward

I computed the configuration-dependent joint-space inertia matrix M(q), Coriolis matrix C(q, q̇), and gravity vector g(q) from the robot model. These terms provide feed-forward torque; stiffness and damping feedback shape the response around the desired trajectory.

τff = M(q)q̈d + C(q, q̇)q̇d + g(q)

τ = Kp(qd − q) + Kd(q̇d − q̇) + τff

M: joint-space inertia matrix · C: Coriolis matrix · g: gravity vector
Kp: stiffness · Kd: damping · d: desired trajectory

User testing

Sidewalk Sidekick / project filmWatch on YouTube

Handle feedback and user interaction

The team tested the prototype with a blind Georgia Tech student across two rounds of evaluation. We evaluated route completion and whether guidance through the handle was understandable, comfortable, and reassuring.

Closely spaced obstacles exposed jitterier motion and reduced perceived safety and clarity. These are formative findings from one participant, not broad validation of an assistive product.

Project context

From Sidewalk Sidekick to ARL.

The capstone application

Sidewalk Sidekick was developed to support navigation for blind and low-vision people through a wheeled-legged base, a rigid guidance handle, and onboard perception. The long-term vision was also to create a reusable research platform for the Aerospace Robotics Lab.

What I am developing next

I am continuing the platform at ARL as Multimodal Mini. The wheels are being modified with grousers for terrain interaction beyond the original sidewalk application, extending the hardware into a longer-term research platform.

Accepted abstract · LSIC

Hierarchical Certified MPPI for Terrain-aware Mode-switching and Control

J. Song, V. Griffo, A. Gandhi, and Y. K. Nakka · Georgia Institute of Technology

A terrain-aware supervisor selects a locomotion mode for a proposed certified MPPI executor. The abstract reports open-loop locomotion demonstrations on Multimodal Mini; closed-loop terrain-aware mode-switching is the next step.

The wider team

Perception, navigation & interaction.

Supporting project context

My work covered the mechanical platform, engineering analysis, impedance controller, and hardware integration. The broader project also included perception, navigation, communications, and human-factors work; those are presented here as team outcomes.

Navigation & remaining work

The team shifted from drift-sensitive global planning toward a depth-based local costmap. The report records a planning response reduction from approximately 2.4 s to 20 ms. Full LiDAR fusion, robust global navigation, and the original curb-climbing targets remained development directions.
