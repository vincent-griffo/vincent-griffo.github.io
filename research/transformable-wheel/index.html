Transformable
Wheel.

A differential wheel for locomotion and manipulation.

The concept combines a rolling wheel with reconfigurable geometry for foot contact and gripping. An internal rotating mechanism changes the configuration within the outer wheel shell, with guide pins locating and locking the states.

My role

Ideation, mentoring, and mechanical integration with the Transformer robot. Gabriele Gargiulo carried out the majority of the mechanism design.

Operating modes

Wheel

The outer shell provides a rolling surface for wheeled locomotion.

Foot

The reconfigured wheel provides contact geometry for legged locomotion.

Gripper

The reconfigured wheel grasps and rotates objects.

Two mechanical configurations support these three uses.

Differential mechanism

Two configurations, three intended uses.

Rolling structure

The wheel shell provides the external rolling surface and contains the reconfiguration mechanism.

Differential actuation

Relative rotation within the shell changes the wheel geometry, allowing it to transition between its mechanical states.

State location and locking

Guide pins constrain the transformation and locate the mechanism so it can lock into its different states.

Contribution and credits

Robot-body integration

My direct engineering work was adapting the wheel to the Transformer robot body. This required structural spline connections capable of carrying high torque, waterjet-cut mounting plates, and the mechanical interfaces between the mechanism and the robot.

Ideation and mentoring

I helped develop the concept and mentored Gabriele during the mechanical design. I brought lessons from my GROVER work, including mistakes from that earlier wheel project, into our design discussions.
