Internship contributions

The work combined packaging constraints with deployment kinematics, prototype development, and electrical integration.

Mechanical design

Transformable fins

I completed a folding-fin design with different deployment approaches at the front and rear. The rear fins folded torsionally; the front fins folded into the chassis. My analysis used eigenvalue decomposition to identify the eigenvector corresponding to an off-principal rotation axis.

Systems integration

Electronics and sensor analysis

My internship also included steering-assembly work, PCB avionics, and sensor trade studies using MATLAB and MODTRAN. This involved mechanical packaging, electronics, and analytical modeling within the same project.
