Experience / Project teamsGeorgia Tech Rover Team

Rover · Aerial systems · Technical leadership

RoboNav.

Rovers for the University Rover Challenge.

RoboNav is Georgia Tech’s rover team for the University Rover Challenge. We reached the international finals in Hanksville, Utah, in both 2024 and 2025.

I served as chief engineer for two years, following my work as drone lead. I am no longer in the role. My contributions included mechanical design, analysis, integration, design reviews, and student mentorship.

2026–2027 · Under construction

Current rover design.

This team assembly is being implemented for 2026–2027; the final rover is still under construction. My contributions included mechanical design review, analysis, and integration.

01 / Mobility

Steering and suspension

Four steerable drive modules and linked suspension support the rover over uneven terrain.

02 / Manipulation

Arm and end effector

A translating base, shoulder, elbow, and differential wrist position the gripper. I contributed to arm sizing and compliant end-effector design.

03 / Integration

Chassis and sensing

The frame carries the arm, camera mast, electronics, and drivetrain. My chief-engineer role connected the subsystem decisions and mechanical interfaces.

Two years as chief engineer · Former role

Design support and technical leadership.

My work expanded from owning aerial hardware to supporting the whole mechanical team. During my two years as chief engineer, I helped students turn concepts and calculations into mechanisms that could be manufactured and integrated.

Subsystem engineering

Design ideas, load calculations, mechanism reviews, and integration support across the rover.

Design reviews

Teaching and mentorship

Held lectures for students and provided ongoing guidance as they developed their own designs.
