About me

I’m pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech, with a minor in Physics and a concentration in Robotics.

I’m interested in multimodal robotics: designing a robot’s morphology, degrees of freedom, and control together to give one platform more ways to move and interact.

I want to pursue a PhD exploring the interplay between robot morphology, control, and physical AI.

I build and test prototypes, from mechanisms and electronics to dynamics and feedback control.

Upcoming: Bhutan. I will be working with Bhutanese officials on a cross-cultural exchange in robotics at Gelephu Mindfulness City.
